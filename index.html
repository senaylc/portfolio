About

I am a Computer Vision and Software Engineer, graduated from Hacettepe University Computer Science in July 2025. Since November 2024 I have been working at EHSİM A.Ş. developing radar simulation software for electronic warfare systems, and leading the company's first AI integration initiative.

My academic research is conducted with Prof. Nazlı İkizler Cinbiş at Hacettepe University. I am co-authoring papers in deepfake detection — applying transformer-based architectures, ViT, Swin Transformers, and CNNs — and in surveillance super-resolution, a project I conceived and proposed independently.

"I aim to become an engineer who designs AI systems end-to-end — from architecture decisions to production deployment — with advanced computer vision as my primary domain."

Alongside my professional role I am advancing my foundations through Coursera's Deep Learning and Reinforcement Learning specialisations, and building two independent projects from scratch: a spatio-temporal radar signal classifier and a computer vision–driven civic mobile application.

Super-Resolution for Surveillance Face Recognition

A research project I proposed and initiated independently — investigating how super-resolution preprocessing can dramatically improve face recognition accuracy on low-quality, low-resolution CCTV footage. Systematically evaluated seven SR models before feeding enhanced frames into a MTCNN + InceptionResNetV2 recognition pipeline. Co-authored academic paper in preparation for journal publication.

  • Evaluated Real-ESRGAN, SwinIR, LapSRN, ESPCN, GPEN, Bicubic as SR preprocessing stages
  • MTCNN face detection and InceptionResNetV2 recognition on enhanced output
  • Systematic benchmark across all model combinations on low-resolution surveillance dataset
  • Best SR + recognition pipeline delivered a 32% accuracy improvement over baseline
